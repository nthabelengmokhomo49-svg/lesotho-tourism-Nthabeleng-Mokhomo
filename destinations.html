Destinations & Activities

Discover the best places and experiences in Lesotho

Adventure

Sani Pass

Dramatic 4×4 mountain pass linking Lesotho to South Africa. Sheer cliffs, hairpin bends and panoramic views. Best experienced with an experienced driver.

  • Half-day / full-day trip
  • Eastern border
Book experience
Nature

Katse Dam

Africa’s second-largest double-curvature arch dam. Guided tours of the dam wall and visitor centre explain the Lesotho Highlands Water Project.

  • 2–3 hours
  • Central highlands
Book experience
Nature

Maletsunyane Falls

A 192-metre single-drop waterfall near Semonkong. Hike to viewpoints or take the world’s longest commercially operated abseil down the face.

  • Half-day
  • Semonkong
Book experience
Culture

Thaba Bosiu

Historic mountain fortress of King Moshoeshoe I. Climb the plateau, visit the royal graves and learn about the founding of the Basotho nation.

  • 2–3 hours
  • Near Maseru
Book experience
Activity

Pony Trekking

Ride sturdy Basotho ponies through remote highland villages and mountain trails. Multi-day treks with overnight stays in traditional huts available.

  • Half-day to multi-day
  • Various locations
Book experience
Culture

Cultural Village Tour

Visit authentic Basotho villages, meet local families, taste traditional food and learn about daily highland life, crafts and customs.

  • Half-day
  • Rural highlands

Highland Hiking

Guided day hikes and multi-day treks through the Maloti Mountains. Routes range from gentle valley walks to challenging high-altitude trails.

  • Day or multi-day
  • Maloti range

Afriski Mountain Resort

  • Northern highlands
